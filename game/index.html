How to play

You are the keeper of the Ember Line. Void shards fall from the dark — shoot them down, catch the pickups they drop, and survive all ten waves. On wave 10 the Hollow Maw rises: break it, and the line burns bright.

  • Move — Arrow keys, WASD, or mouse
  • Shoot — hold Space, Z, or the mouse button
  • Bomb — B (clears the sky, hurts everything)
  • Pause — P  ·  Mute — M  ·  Restart — R

Kills in a row raise your combo multiplier, up to ×5 — but one hit resets it. Spread shot, shield, extra life and bombs drop from shards.
